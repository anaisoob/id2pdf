# Test data

`empty.png`, `card.png` and `two_cards.png` are scans made for id2pdf with the printed test
cards from `docs/test_cards.pdf`; they are under id2pdf's own licence.

The `photo_*.jpg` files are phone photos of mock identity documents from
[MIDV-2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.00396) (K. Bulatov, E. Emelianova, D. Tropin et al.,
Computer Optics 46(2), 2022), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). The documents are not real
and the faces were generated by [Generated Photos](https://generated.photos/). The photos were
scaled down to 1200 pixels and turned upright; `photo_no_card.jpg` is the top of
`photo_table.jpg`. They are shared under the same licence.
